Tactet bills through Stripe. The account owner sees every invoice in the UI, under Billing › Invoices.

  • Invoices are issued in the first week of each month and are paid on Stripe’s website.
  • The current invoice shows the charges so far and a projection for the end of the month; past invoices show what was charged.

For billing questions, write to billing@tactet.com with your account ID. Prices are on the Pricing page.
