Search2o bills through Stripe. The account owner sees every invoice in the GUI, under Billing › Invoices.

  • Invoices are issued in the first week of each month.
  • The per-user charge is prorated by the days each user was on the account.
  • The platform fee starts with the second month.
  • The current invoice shows the charges so far and a projection for the end of the month; past invoices show what was charged.

For billing questions, please write to billing@search2o.com with your account ID. Prices are on the Pricing page.
