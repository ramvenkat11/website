Search2o is in free, open beta, and usage limits help us protect the resources behind the service. Limits are counted daily, on the UTC day.

What sets your limits

An account's limits follow its service level.

LevelWhat it suits
FreeGetting to know Search2o on your own. These are the smallest limits, and evaluating with many users may feel tight.
EvalEvaluating Search2o across an organization, with many users. The limits are considerably higher than the free limits.
PaidNormal operation without interruption. The limits follow the number of users on the account.

Why the numbers are not published

We do not publish the limits themselves. We expect to adjust them as the beta goes on, and a number published here would soon be the wrong one.

If you reach a limit

Reaching a limit shows an error message in the GUI.

Asking for higher limits

If the limits are getting in your way, we would be glad to hear from you, and we can raise them. Please tell us about your company and your position in it, since that is what we need in order to consider the request. If the request is urgent, please say so. Support describes the ways to reach us.
