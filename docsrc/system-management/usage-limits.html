Usage limits help us protect the resources behind the service. Each plan comes with monthly allowances for agent executions, agent indexing, searches and AI assistance; the allowances are on the Pricing page.

What sets your limits

An account's allowances follow its plan. On the Free plan the allowances are per account. On the Paid plan they grow with the number of members and are pooled across the account, so what one member does not use is available to the others.

If you reach a limit

Reaching a limit shows an error message in the GUI. On the Free plan, the limit holds until the month resets. On the Paid plan, usage beyond a pooled allowance continues and is billed at the rates on the Pricing page. If you see a limit you did not expect, please reach out to us.
