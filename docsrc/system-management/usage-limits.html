Usage limits help us protect the resources behind the service. Limits are counted daily, on the UTC day.

What sets your limits

An account's limits follow its service level.

LevelWhat it suits
IndividualA single user getting to know Search2o. These are the smallest limits.
EvaluationAn organization-wide evaluation, with many users. The limits are considerably higher than the Individual limits.
TeamNormal operation without interruption. The limits follow the number of users on the account.

Why the numbers are not published

We do not publish the limits themselves. We expect to adjust them as the beta goes on, and a number published here would soon be the wrong one.

If you reach a limit

Reaching a limit shows an error message in the GUI. If you see this as a paying customer, please reach out to us.
