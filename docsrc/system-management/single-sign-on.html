Search2o authenticates with email and password today. Single sign-on is on our roadmap, and we would welcome your input on it.

What we expect to support

We expect to support SAML 2.0 and OpenID Connect. Either would let your company keep its own identity provider as the place where people sign in, so a person could reach Search2o with the account you already manage.

Until then, sign-in is the built-in email and password described in Authentication, with the session and password rules an administrator sets.

We would like to hear from you

What our customers need will guide what we build first. If single sign-on matters to your organization, we would be glad to know which standard and which identity provider you use.

We would also welcome a pilot. If your company is open to working with us on one, your requirements would help shape what we build.

The Help icon in the GUI reaches us, and so does info@search2o.com. See Support.

What we expect to keep the same

We intend to keep roles in Search2o. An identity provider would say who a person is, and Search2o would still decide what that person may do. We also expect integration tokens and the connect flow for a chat bot to work as they do today.
