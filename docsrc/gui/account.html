Two groups: Admin — Users, Authentication and License, for administrators and the owner — and Billing — Account and Invoices, for the owner.

Users

Everyone in the account, with their role. Filter the list by role. Add users by email — from the Evaluation level up; an Individual account has a single user. Each new user receives an email, and sets a password through New user / Forgot password? on the sign-in dialog. Change a user's role, or delete users, from this page. See Users and roles.

Authentication

The session cookie, the password policy, session lifetimes, the allowed email domain, and the emails sent to users. See Authentication.

License

The account name and three administrator actions: license key rotation, encryption key rotation, and the account freeze.

Account

The billing side of the account, for the owner: request the upgrade to the Evaluation level, or delete the account. You are notified of the Evaluation approval by email within one business day. Deleting the account is permanent.

Invoices

Itemized invoices with prorated charges, current and past. The current invoice shows the charges accumulated so far and a projection for the end of the month; past invoices show what was charged.
