Administrator pages for the agent servers and the systems they connect to.

Agent servers

The named server configurations: paths, CORS, logging, and the cloud connection pool. The page also shows what each running server reported when the server loaded the configuration: allowlist entries that failed to import, and adapters that failed to instantiate. See Agent servers.

Connection pools

Named HTTP pools with their connection limits and timeouts. See Connection pools.

Secrets & encryption

Where sys.secret reads from, and who holds the encryption key. The two settings are saved separately. Hosted secrets are entered here and are encrypted with your own key before they are stored. See Secrets and Encryption.
