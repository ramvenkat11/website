# Support

During open beta, Search2o support is available by email.

For product support, deployment questions, licensing, legal, or other inquiries, contact:

[info@search2o.com](mailto:info@search2o.com)

We respond during business hours on working days.

For product-related issues, please email us from the same email address you used to register for your Search2o license.

Before general availability, a full-featured help desk will be integrated directly into the Search2o application.
