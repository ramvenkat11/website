Since Search2o is in free, open beta, we have usage limits to protect our resources. 

usage limits are enforced on a daily basis, based on UTC day

The limits are set based on whether the account is free, eval or paid

Free has the minimum limits. It can be quite limited to evaluate with many users. It is more suited to get used to the product, individually. 

Eval has much higher limits and is suited for evaluation for your organization with many users. 

Paid's limits are based on number of users and is set for normal operations without interruption. 

We do not publish the actual limits as these limits will be adjusted as required. 

If you hit one of these limits, you will see an error message in the UI. 

You can contact us to increase your limits. Please specify enough details about your company and your position in your message to us. If the request is urgent, please mention it.
