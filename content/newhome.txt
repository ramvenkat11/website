Search2o agent server runs in your organization. We do not charge a license fee for this server.

Search2o agent server uses Search2o cloud for state management. We will charge a platform fee for this service.

We are currently in beta and offer the platform free of charge.

The production pricing will have a platform fee, per developer fee, a storage fee based on agents, memories and conversations stored and a usage fee that is based on calls made to the Search2o cloud. It will not have a per user fee and so you will pay for what you use. You can expect the rates to be similar to AI services in the market. Currently, the plan is to offer paid services in Jan 2027.

There are usage limits per account. If they get in your way, let us know and we'll increase them.
