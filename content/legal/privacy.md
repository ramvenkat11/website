# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: September 5, 2026**

This Privacy Policy describes how Search2o, Inc. ("Search2o," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, discloses, and retains personal information in connection with our website, software, cloud services, and related services (collectively, the "Service").

The Service is intended for business and professional use by individuals who are at least 18 years old.

This Privacy Policy describes our privacy practices and does not create contractual rights beyond those provided by applicable law or an express written agreement with Search2o.

## 1. Information We Collect

Depending on how the Service is used, we may collect or process:

* **Account information**, including name, email address, account name, role, permissions, authentication information, and account settings.
* **Usage and technical information**, including IP addresses, requested URLs, timestamps, login and access activity, agents executed, execution results, errors, performance information, resource usage, other operational information, and, for website interactions, browser or device information.
* **Customer Content**, meaning queries, conversations, conversation state, long-term memories, agent definitions and descriptions, drafts, configurations, profiles, and other information submitted to or generated through the Service.
* **Communications**, including information provided when contacting Search2o for support or otherwise communicating with us.

Except for website browsing and registration, ordinary use of the Service occurs through an agent server operated by the customer.

If you voluntarily post information to a public issue tracker, discussion forum, or other public service, that information is public.

## 2. How We Use Information

We may use information as reasonably necessary to:

* provide, operate, maintain, and administer the Service;
* create and manage accounts and authenticate users;
* perform searches and provide requested functionality;
* maintain conversations, agents, and other Service data;
* provide reports and manage usage;
* allocate and manage computing resources;
* provide support and Service-related communications;
* monitor, secure, troubleshoot, and maintain the reliability and performance of the Service;
* detect, investigate, and prevent fraud, abuse, unauthorized access, and security incidents;
* enforce licenses, usage limits, agreements, and policies;
* comply with legal, regulatory, tax, accounting, and other obligations;
* establish, exercise, or defend legal rights; and
* evaluate or complete a merger, financing, acquisition, reorganization, sale of assets, or similar transaction.

Customer Content is processed as necessary to provide, operate, secure, support, and administer the Service, comply with law, enforce applicable agreements, and protect Search2o, its customers, and others.

Search2o does not sell personal information, provide personal information to data brokers, or use personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising.

## 3. Customer Responsibility

Customers are responsible for Customer Content and for obtaining all rights, permissions, notices, and consents required to collect, submit, use, disclose, and process Customer Content through the Service.

Search2o does not determine what personal information a customer's agents request from or receive from users.

Customers are responsible for determining whether the Service is appropriate for the information they choose to process and for complying with laws applicable to their Customer Content and use of the Service.

If you use Search2o through an employer or other organization, that organization may administer your account and may have access to information concerning your account and use of the Service, including your name, email address, role, account activity, agents executed, usage information, and reports.

Privacy requests relating to information controlled by your organization should generally be directed to that organization.

## 4. Artificial Intelligence and External Services

Certain Search2o features use third-party artificial intelligence service providers. Information may be transmitted to those providers as necessary to perform functions such as agent indexing, documentation assistance, and agent-development assistance.

**Do not submit sensitive personal information through documentation questions, agent-writing assistance, or similar AI-assistance features.**

Customers may configure agents running on their agent servers to communicate with third-party LLM providers, APIs, databases, applications, MCP servers, and other external systems.

Those communications originate from the customer's agent server rather than Search2o Cloud. Customers are responsible for selecting, configuring, and using those services and for determining whether their use complies with applicable requirements.
