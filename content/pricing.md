# Pricing

Search2o is available as an public beta, with no charge during the beta period.

The agent server runs inside your environment. Search2o Cloud provides search, governance, state management, reporting, and system control.

All capabilities are available during beta.

---

## Beta access

* No charge during beta
* Organization-level deployments supported
* Evaluation, agent development, and real workflows supported
* Fair-use limits apply

The beta is not intended for load testing, stress testing, high-volume benchmarking, or artificial traffic generation.

---

## After beta

Pricing will be announced before general availability.

Beta users will receive advance notice before any paid plan begins, with optional migration to a paid plan and early-customer pricing.

Pricing is expected to be based primarily on active use, with no high fixed platform costs.
