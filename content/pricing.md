# Pricing

Search2o is currently in public beta. Individual and team evaluations are free. Paid service will be offered soon.

## Individual

**Free**

For one person evaluating Search2o on a local machine.

* 1 user
* 1-month evaluation period
* All product capabilities included
* Tight daily usage limits
* Intended for local evaluation and agent development
* Upgrade to Team Evaluation or Team to continue after the evaluation period

## Team Evaluation

**Free**

For organizations evaluating Search2o with a shared deployment.

* Up to 100 users
* 2-month evaluation period, beginning when the account is upgraded to Team Evaluation
* All product capabilities included
* Significantly higher usage limits than Individual
* Intended for shared deployments and real organizational evaluation
* Existing agents, configuration, and account data carry over from Individual
* Upgrade to Team to continue after the evaluation period

Request Team Evaluation from the Account page.

## Team

**Coming soon**

For ongoing organizational use.

**$100/month platform fee**
**+$40/user/month**

* All product capabilities included
* Usage limits scale with the number of users
* Shared account-level usage limits
* User charges are prorated when users are added or removed
* Existing agents, configuration, and account data carry over from Individual or Team Evaluation

Paid service will be introduced before the end of 2026. Pricing is published now so organizations can evaluate Search2o with a clear understanding of expected ongoing cost.
