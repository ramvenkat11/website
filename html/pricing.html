Pricing

The agent server is free

Search2o agent server runs in your organization. It is source available and there is no license fee for it.

Search2o Cloud is free during the beta

The agent server uses Search2o Cloud to manage state. There will be a charge for this service, when it is generally available. Currently, the platform is in beta and it is free of charge.

General availability pricing

General availability pricing tentatively will have four parts: a platform fee, a fee per developer, a storage fee based on units stored, and a usage fee based on calls to the cloud. There will be no fee per user, so you pay for what you use. You can expect these rates to be similar to other AI platforms in the market.

Usage limits

Your free account has usage limits. If they get in your way, send us a message from the UI, and we will raise them.

Switching to the paid service

When the paid service is available, you will be able to upgrade from the Account page. Currently, we plan to offer the paid service in January 2027.

Tell us what you need

We would love to help you make your project a success. If you have questions, find a bug or miss a feature, tell us. When you are signed in, send a message from the message icon in the UI. Before you have an account, use the contact page.
