Describe an agent and AI will draft it

Draft with AI writes or edits an agent from your description. In the product, it is available in the agent editor.

Search2o agents follow a defined schema, so the AI produces a fully formed agent. It does not write the prompts or the profiles: both are defined outside the agent, and the draft refers to them by name.
